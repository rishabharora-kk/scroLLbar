Experiment 01 · Retrieval rather than orientation

Find what you half remember.

An index should reveal what is not on screen. Compare ordinary scrolling and a Contents outline against a preview rail that reveals off-screen figures, tables and passages without moving the document.

How it works

Grab the blue thumb on the right and drag. The small card previews a distinct landmark elsewhere in the document; the document stays put until release. Move your finger at least 150px inward and release to cancel. After jumping, choose Back to return exactly where you started. Toggle the preview rail off for the baseline.
